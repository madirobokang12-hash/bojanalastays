Questions owners ask

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. There is no setup fee and no monthly fee. We take a share of the profit after costs such as platform fees, cleaning, laundry, supplies and approved repairs. We agree the split with you in writing before we start.

Do you guarantee income?

No. Income depends on location, condition, pricing and demand. We show you real comparable listings before you decide, so your expectations are realistic.

What if a guest damages my property?

We screen guests, set clear house rules and photograph the property at every turnover. If something is damaged we tell you within 24 hours and claim through the booking platform and your insurance where they apply.

Can I still use my property myself?

Yes. Give us 14 days' notice for the dates you want to block, subject to bookings already confirmed.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. We start with a 3-month trial. After that, either of us can end the agreement with 30 days' notice.

Are short-term rentals allowed at my property?

It depends on zoning, your body corporate or HOA rules and your lease if you rent the property yourself. Please check before we start. We can help you ask the right questions.
